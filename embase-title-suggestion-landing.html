Is your journal a fit?

Our evaluation team reviews each submission against the same criteria below. Review these before you submit.

Journal quality

  • Has a full, documented editorial board
  • Is peer reviewed
  • Publishes full-text articles in English
  • Includes reference lists in its articles

About the journal

  • Published periodically with an ISSN
  • Available in electronic format
  • A publication ethics statement is publicly available
  • Editorial policy is clear, and the website is in English

Meeting these criteria isn't enough on its own: your journal's subject area also needs to fall within one of Embase's current priority coverage areas. A high-quality journal outside those subject areas won't be selected.

Before you start A few situations mean it's not the right time to submit:
  • It was rejected by Scopus in the last 24 months — wait 24 months from that decision
  • You submitted to Embase in the last 24 months and are still waiting, or were previously rejected
